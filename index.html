A 4-week plan

  • Week 1: Arrays, hashing, two pointers, sliding window, stacks. 2–3 problems a day.
  • Week 2: Linked lists, trees, heaps, binary search. Watch each visualizer before solving.
  • Week 3: Graphs, backtracking, DP. Start one system design every two days.
  • Week 4: Mock interviews, behavioral stories (aim for 8), redo missed problems.

Common bugs

  • Off-by-one: decide whether hi is inclusive or exclusive and keep it that way.
  • Use mid = lo + (hi - lo) // 2 in fixed-width languages to avoid overflow.
  • Infinite loop when lo = mid and mid rounds down.

Behavioral

Build a bank of STAR stories. Pick a question, write your story, then time yourself saying it aloud (aim for about 2 minutes).

What interviewers listen for

  • Spend about 60% on Action. Keep Situation and Task short (about 20%) and finish with Result (about 20%).
  • Say "I", not "we". They are hiring you, not your team.
  • Put numbers on results: latency down 40%, saved 10 hours a week, unblocked 3 teams.
  • For failure questions, show what you learned and what you changed afterwards.
  • Prepare 6–8 strong stories that each fit several questions (conflict, ownership, failure, impact).
  • Amazon maps questions to its Leadership Principles. Google looks at "Googleyness" and leadership. Meta focuses on conflict, growth and impact.
